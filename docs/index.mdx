**keepalive** is a pip-installable watchdog for GPU training runs. It watches your
W&B metrics, catches failures (NaN loss, divergence, stalls, OOM, crashes), explains
what went wrong in plain English, messages you on Telegram, and — if you don't answer in time — takes
over: it spawns parallel coding agents that each write a different fix, races those
fixes as short checkpoint-resumed training runs, and promotes the one with the lowest
loss. Every decision is traced in [W&B Weave](https://wandb.ai/site/weave).

> Agents that hold you accountable — and stop waiting when you don't show up.

## The escalation ladder

keepalive escalates along three rungs. Each rung is a different relationship between
you and the system.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Awareness" icon="eye">
    Detection + diagnosis. The watchdog notices the failure the instant it happens
    and turns it into a plain-English explanation — no more staring at loss curves.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agency" icon="bell">
    Escalation. You get a Telegram message with inline buttons plus an AI voice note.
    Tap **⏪ Roll back**, **🔧 Apply fix**, or **🛑 Stop** (or type 1/2/3). You stay in control.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Assurance" icon="shield">
    Autonomy. If you don't reply before the deadline, authority transfers. Coding
    agents write fixes, keepalive races them, and the winner becomes your training.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The 30-second pitch

Training runs fail at 3am. By the time you wake up you've burned eight hours of GPU
time on a diverged run. keepalive sits inside your training loop, catches the failure
in-process (zero lag), diagnoses it with GPT, and messages you on Telegram. If you're asleep, it
doesn't just sit there — it branches your repo at the exact failing commit, hands the
problem to parallel Cursor cloud agents that each propose a fix, runs each fix as a
short probe from your last good checkpoint on W&B Sandboxes, and keeps the one that
actually drives loss down. You wake up to a recovered run and a shareable Weave trace
of every decision.
